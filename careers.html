Roadmap

How to become a commercial pilot

Becoming a pilot in India follows a clear sequence. We guide you at every step.

Eligibility

Class 12 with Physics & Maths, age 17+.

Medicals

DGCA Class 2, then Class 1 medical.

Computer Number

Get your DGCA Computer Number.

Ground classes

Clear the DGCA theory papers.

Flying hours

Train at a flying school in India or abroad.

CPL & airline

Get your CPL, type rating, then join an airline.
