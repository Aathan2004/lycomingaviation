About the Commercial Pilot Licence course

Airlines are preparing for their next round of hiring, which will create thousands of new jobs for pilots, cabin crew and ground staff.

To meet this demand, Lycoming Aviation offers CPL Ground Classes at a cost-effective price for students who want to build a career in airlines. The course follows DGCA regulations.

Eligibility

Class 12 pass with Physics and Mathematics (you can clear these through NIOS if needed), or a Diploma or Degree. Minimum age to start is 17.

Under DGCA regulation: CPL ground classes are taught by CPL holders, following the DGCA Section 7 Flight Crew Standards, Training and Licensing series, Part IV syllabus.
  • Our course contents include
  • Air Navigation
  • Air Regulation
  • Aviation Meteorology
  • Basic RTR
  • Technical Specifics
  • Uniform (included)
  • Book materials (included)
  • Flight simulator, 10 hrs (included)

About NIOS assistance

To start pilot training in India you need Physics and Mathematics at Class 12 level. If you studied a different stream, you can clear both subjects through NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling). We guide you through the whole process so you become eligible for a Commercial Pilot Licence.

  • How we help
  • NIOS registration & subject selection
  • Study guidance for Physics & Maths
  • Exam schedule & documentation
  • Start DGCA ground classes alongside
