India or abroad

Two routes to a DGCA CPL.

Both pathways end in a DGCA Commercial Pilot Licence. They differ in timeline, cost and how the licence reaches your hand.

Compare on🇮🇳 Flight training in IndiaASET Flying School, Thoothukudi🌍 Flight training abroad7 partner countries
Licence issued byDGCA — issued directlyForeign regulator (FAA, CASA, GCAA, EASA…)
ConversionNot requiredDGCA conversion on your return — we guide you
Flying conditionsWeather and traffic can stretch the calendarOften more flying days, faster hour building
LocationClose to home, in Tamil NaduOverseas, visa required
Best suited forStudents who want to stay near family and avoid conversionStudents optimising for speed and international exposure

Which should you choose?

Train abroad for faster hour building if you can manage a conversion. Train in India for a licence issued directly and to stay close to home. Our counsellors compare both for your budget and timeline.

FAQ

Questions students ask us.

Who can apply for pilot training?

Students who have passed Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics and are at least 17 years old. If you studied a different stream, you can clear Physics and Maths through NIOS — we'll guide you.

How long does it take to become a commercial pilot?

Usually around 18 months to 2 years, depending on ground exam results, flying-school schedules and weather.

Is an education loan available?

Many banks offer loans for pilot training. Our counsellors help you prepare the documents and plan the payment stages.

Do you help with jobs after the course?

Yes. We offer placement assistance for all programmes, including interview and grooming preparation.

I didn't take Physics and Maths in Class 12. Can I still become a pilot?

Yes. You can clear Physics and Maths through NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling). Our NIOS assistance covers registration, subject selection, study guidance and documentation.

What is eGCA and do you help with it?

eGCA is DGCA's online portal for pilot registration, logbook entries and licence applications. We handle eGCA registration, logbook entries and RTR, FRTOL, CPL and extension-of-rating applications for you.

Can you arrange type rating slots?

Yes. We arrange A320, B737 and ATR type rating slots with training partners. Ask us for current availability and fees.

Can I visit the academy before joining?

Of course. Book a counselling call below and we'll fix a time for you and your parents to visit.
