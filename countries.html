🇮🇳 Pilot training in India

Fly in Thoothukudi

Train at ASET Flying School, Thoothukudi, powered by Lycoming Aviation. Your licence is issued directly by DGCA, so there is no conversion step.

  • Flying school: ASET Flying School, Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu
  • Powered by Lycoming Aviation
  • Regulator: DGCA
  • No conversion needed: your licence is Indian from day one.
  • Costs, duration and next batch: on request

🇵🇭 Pilot training in Philippines

Fly in the Philippines

An affordable, English-speaking option close to India, with good year-round flying weather, under CAAP, followed by DGCA conversion.

  • Regulator: CAAP (Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines)
  • Partner training centre through Lycoming
  • After training, we guide your licence conversion through DGCA so you can fly for Indian airlines.
  • Costs, duration and next batch: on request

India vs abroad

Which is right for you?

🇮🇳 Training in India

Licence issued directly by DGCA, no conversion step, and close to home. Flying days can depend on weather and aircraft availability.

🌍 Training abroad

Often more flying days and international exposure. You'll need DGCA licence conversion afterwards, and we guide you through it.
