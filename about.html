About Lycoming Aviation Academy

A fast-growing pilot training organisation in South India, founded by aviation professionals in 2021.

Our story

From pilots to a growing family.

Lycoming Aviation Academy began in 2021, when seven young Indian Tamil pilots set out to give aspiring aviators the honest guidance and quality training they once had to search hard for. Today more than 30 pilots are part of our network, and every student who joins becomes part of the Lycoming family, supported from the first class all the way to the cockpit.

Our founder

Sai Prasanth G

Founder & Managing Director

At the helm of Lycoming is Mr. Sai Prasanth G, who has shaped the academy's growth and reputation. Under his leadership, the academy has become a hub where aspiring aviators get hands-on training, modern facilities and internationally aligned flight programmes.

The leadership team brings years of flying experience and industry insight, so every student learns technical skills along with the professionalism and responsibility that define great pilots. Our mission isn't just to train pilots. It's to nurture future aviation leaders who can confidently navigate global skies.
