🩺 Class 1 & Class 2 medicals

Every pilot needs a DGCA Class 2 medical to start, and a Class 1 medical before the CPL is issued. We book your appointments with DGCA-approved examiners and tell you exactly what to bring.

  • Class 1 medical appointment

Registration

🪪 DGCA Computer Number

The Computer Number is a unique ID that DGCA assigns to pilots and aspiring pilots in India. You need it before you can sit any DGCA exam or apply for a CPL. We prepare and file your application.

  • What we handle
  • 10th & 12th mark sheets (CBSE & STATE BOARD)
  • Aadhaar (e-Aadhaar) & passport
  • Board verification (STATE BOARD)
  • Photo with white background
  • Signature on a white A4 sheet
  • Enrolment form (filled in CAPITALS)
FeesFees Available on Request
Time50–70 days

eGCA portal

💻 eGCA registration

eGCA is DGCA's online portal for pilot records and licence applications. We set up your eGCA profile correctly the first time, with every document in order.

  • What we handle
  • Profile creation on eGCA
  • Document upload and verification

  • Help with portal queries
