Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Draft pending legal review. This page was prepared to explain how this website works. It has not yet been reviewed by an Indian lawyer. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

Lycoming Aviation Academy India Pvt Ltd (“Lycoming Aviation”, “we”, “us”) runs this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect through it, why, and the choices you have. It is written to be consistent with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

What we collect

Only what you type into the Apply Now form, which is:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone / WhatsApp number and country code
  • Your city (optional)
  • The course you are interested in
  • Your message (optional)

We do not ask for identity documents, marksheets, Aadhaar, passport or bank details through this website. Please do not send them in the message box. If we need documents later for an eGCA or licence application, we will tell you what is needed and how to share it.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and call you back about the course you asked about.
  • To give you accurate information about courses, services and flight training options.
  • To keep a record of the enquiry so we do not ask you the same questions twice.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry without asking you first.

How the form works

This website has no database. When you press Send on WhatsApp, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message prepared from what you typed, addressed to our WhatsApp number. The message is sent only when you press send inside WhatsApp, and it is then handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Calls and messages

By sending an enquiry you agree that we may contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about it. Tell us at any time if you prefer not to be contacted, or want a different method, and we will stop.

Other services this website uses

  • Google Fonts and a Google Maps embed (Contact page): Google may receive your IP address and device information when these load.
  • Netlify hosts the website and keeps ordinary server logs (such as IP address and pages requested).
  • Instagram, WhatsApp and Google links take you to their services, which have their own policies.

See our Cookie Policy for what is stored on your device.

Keeping your information safe

Enquiry messages are kept only in our WhatsApp account and phone, which are protected by the usual access controls. We ask team members to use enquiry details only to respond to you. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise otherwise.

How long we keep it

[OWNER TO CONFIRM: retention period, for example “until your enquiry is resolved, and no longer than 12 months afterwards”.]

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to being contacted. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should send the enquiry or tell us they agree. Write to the contact below; see also our grievance page.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
